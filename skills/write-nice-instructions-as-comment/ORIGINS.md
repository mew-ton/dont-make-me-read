# Where these rules came from, and what sits next to them

Nothing points at this file, and nothing should: it is provenance and neighbourhood for whoever
maintains the skill, not material an agent writing a finding has to load. Keeping it here keeps it
out of the body, where it would cost every reader who is only trying to write a comment.

## Where this skill sits among its neighbours

Most of these the skill no longer names, because naming them changed nothing an author does next.
Where one still appears, it is on the step it changes. They are recorded here for whoever edits the
skill.

- `dont-make-me-read` — the test this one sharpens: the finding is judged by what its reader has to
  hold before they can act.
- `avoid-unverifiable-context` — whether the reader can check what the finding claims: the rationale
  stays, the process it came out of goes.
- `avoid-reference-detours` — whether the reader was sent somewhere to understand the finding, and
  what may be restated at the point of use instead.
- `write-nice-issue-description` — a problem statement plus a completion condition, read by an
  implementer who can read the repository. This skill stops where that one starts: the issue opened
  by a finding with no nameable fix is written to that skill's rules, not to this template. The body
  says the issue is not this template's job without naming the skill, since it does not invoke it.
- `write-nice-instructions-as-rule` — the same demand where the instruction stays in force: a
  guideline, a convention, or the rules inside a skill body, read out of order by whoever the rule
  catches. The description's bound takes that case back without naming the skill.

## What the guards are a record of

Common Rationalizations and Red Flags are filled from what was actually said and what was actually
found in a draft. A row is the record of an excuse someone had to answer; deleting one to save
tokens deletes the record and leaves the cause.
